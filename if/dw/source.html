Section - The Magical Language

An element is a kind of value. The elements are neutral, earthy, airy, firey, and watery. A thing has an element. The element of a thing is usually neutral.

A magic noun is a kind of value. Some magic nouns are defined by the Table of Magic Nouns.

Table of Magic Nouns

magic noun meaning

shrad earthy

To decide which object is the focus of (M - a magic noun):

repeat with T running through visible things:

if T is not the player and the element of T is the meaning of M:

decide on T;

decide on nothing.

Understand "IGTAP [magic noun]" as destroying.

Destroying is an action applying to one magic noun.

The destroying action has an object called the spell target.

Setting action variables for destroying:

now the spell target is the focus of the magic noun understood.

Check destroying:

if the spell target is nothing:

say "You feel a rush of undirected power, which dissipates immediately."

Carry out destroying:

now the spell target is nowhere.

Report destroying:

say "You feel a rush of power, and [the spell target] disintegrates into a cloud of [if the element of the spell target is earthy]dust.[otherwise]pure magic."

Part 1 - The First Level

The Foyer is a room. "An ancient tablet inscribed with runes stands before you."

In the Foyer is The Ancient Tablet. The Ancient Tablet is scenery. "You translate the runes, referring to your painstakingly researched notes. The runes read 'IGTAP SHRAD'."

The rockfall is in the Foyer. "A pile of rocks blocks passage further into the temple." The rockfall is earthy.
